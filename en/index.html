Contact

For questions, problems or ideas, write to traveltoapp1@gmail.com. If a flight imported from Gmail is wrong or missing, attach a screenshot of the confirmation email (you can cover your name and booking codes).

Frequently asked questions

Google says "Google hasn't verified this app". Is it safe?

Yes. TravelTo is in beta and Google's verification will come with the official release. Tap Advanced, then Go to TravelTo. The app only asks to read your emails and analyses them on your iPhone: they are not sent to any server.

Why do I have to reconnect Gmail every week?

During the beta Google ends the access after 7 days. The app shows "Reconnect Gmail" and, once you sign in again, the search picks up where it stopped.

How do I disconnect Gmail?

In the app: Import from Gmail → Sign out. Or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How do I delete my data?

Your data stays only on your iPhone. You can delete flights from the Flights list ("Delete all flights"), remove a country from its card by tapping the selected status again, or delete the app to erase everything.

I bought Flights from Gmail on another iPhone

Open the purchase screen and tap Restore purchases with the same Apple ID.
