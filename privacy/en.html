TravelTo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

TravelTo is an iPhone app that helps you keep track of the countries you have visited and the flights you have taken. This page explains which data the app uses, where it stays and who it is shared with.

In short

  • Your data stays on your iPhone. TravelTo has no server of its own and does not collect data about you.
  • If you connect Gmail, the app reads emails read-only and analyses them on the device. Emails are never sent to us or to third parties.
  • No ads, no tracking, no profiling, no sale of data.

Data stored on the device

Visited countries and states, trips with departure and return dates, notes, profiles, friends you add yourself, and flights. This data is stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. You can delete it at any time from the app or by deleting the app.

Gmail connection (optional)

If you choose to connect your Google account, TravelTo asks for the gmail.readonly permission (read-only), plus your email address to show which account is connected and your first and last name, used only on the device to recognise bookings made for other people.

  • What it reads: only emails that look like flight confirmations, boarding passes or travel receipts, with their PDF attachments.
  • What it extracts: airports, dates, times and flight numbers, saved on your iPhone as flights.
  • Where the analysis happens: entirely on the device, including when the app uses Apple Intelligence to tell real bookings from offers. Email content never leaves your iPhone.
  • What it does not do: it does not send, change or delete emails; it keeps no copies of emails; it does not use Gmail data for advertising or to train AI models; it does not transfer Gmail data to anyone.
  • Credentials: the Google access token is stored in your iPhone's Keychain.
  • Revoking access: disconnect Gmail at any time with "Esci" in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TravelTo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services

  • Google (sign-in and Gmail API), only if you connect Gmail.
  • adsbdb.com: to fill in the connections of a trip, the app sends only a flight number (for example "MU788") to look up its route. No names, emails, dates or other personal data are sent.
  • AeroDataBox (through RapidAPI): for flights in the last year the app may send a flight number and date (for example "MU788, 19/03/2026") to look up that day's airports and times. No names, emails or other personal data are sent.
  • Apple: Maps for the map, the App Store for in-app purchases, on-device Apple Intelligence. These services are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. TravelTo does not receive your payment details.

Children

TravelTo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Your rights

Since your data stays on your device, you are in control: you can view, edit and delete it directly in the app. For any question, write to the address below.
